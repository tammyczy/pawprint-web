THE IDEA

Not one pet’s world.
Every pet’s story.

When a pet dies, the grief can be as deep as losing a person, yet it is rarely taken seriously. Pawprint offers a way to remember them on your own terms. The companion is an AI simulation, not the real pet, and its effects on grief have not been established.

Pawprint is a creation service for pet owners. Give your companion a name, upload their photos, and follow the journey from familiar face to interactive 3D model. A video and a recording of their real sounds can be kept alongside the project as private references.

Current pilot: photos drive 3D generation. Video and sound are saved as references, not automatically turned into animation or cloned audio. Each generated model still needs review.

WHY IT MATTERS

A real grief,
rarely recognized.

Pet owners and families experience loss in different ways. Pawprint explores personal ways to remember a companion.

01

94 million homes

U.S. households that live with at least one pet. Nearly every one will one day say goodbye. APPA, 2025

02

Pet loss can be profound

In a UK study of adults who identified pet loss as their most distressing bereavement, 7.5% reported symptoms consistent with prolonged grief. This does not estimate the rate among young people. Hyland, PLOS ONE, 2026

03

Not recognized

Current diagnostic guidelines do not allow prolonged grief disorder to be diagnosed after a pet dies, and mourners often hear “it was just a pet.” Source

OUR PROMISES

Remembering,
honestly.

Researchers have raised concerns about AI “griefbots”, including dependence and commercial exploitation. Pawprint is designed to let people choose when to engage; its effects have not been established.

Not a resurrection

Your companion is an AI simulation. It is designed not to claim it is your real pet or knows its thoughts, but AI responses can make mistakes.

No pull to come back

No notifications, unsolicited “I miss you” messages or streaks. You choose when to visit.

Your photos, your choice

Location data is removed, and nothing is sent for 3D generation without your consent.

Support you can choose

In the companion experience, you can open a support card to find ways to reach people you trust. Pawprint is an AI simulation and does not replace professional or crisis support.

FROM PHOTO TO PRESENCE

Made to feel
alive.

Three layers come together to create an experience that is personal, expressive, and easy to explore.

01

Your pet’s look

Photos guide a textured 3D model, with coat color and recognizable details treated as part of its identity.

02

Movement with character

After rigging and review, supported walks and other motions turn a still model into a companion you can play with.

03

A voice of its own

Speak or type, choose a voice, and hear your companion reply. Lanlao shows how natural AI speech and on-screen words can work together.
